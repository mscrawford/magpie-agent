## 3. The three numbers, each with its caveat

### Gate detection: **42.9%** (21/49 raw)

Up from **32.7%** raw on 2026-07-20. Verified non-circular: the harness's newest
contributing fix commit is 2026-07-18, so none of this week's work is in the bug corpus —
only the detector changed.

> **Compare against 32.7%, not 30.4%.** 30.4% was the banner-adjusted figure; mixing
> bases would overstate the gain.

Still structurally blind on four classes: `data_source` 0/2, `attribution_role` 0/2,
`citation` 0/2, `mechanism` 0/1.

### Flywheel detection: **6/7** on exactly those blind spots

Positive control 1/1; total 7/8. **The two detectors are complementary, not redundant.**

> **Not a rate.** n=8, pre-registered, deliberately stratified toward the gate's blind
> spots. And the R59 confound is reproduced here: each auditor was told which *class* to
> attend to, and an auditor told where to look finds more. A lead for a matched round.
> Cost belongs beside it: ~100–155k tokens per audit versus seconds for the whole gate.

### Mutation survival: **45.6%** (502/1101), against a <5% bar

Roster extended from 24 to 29 checkers, because five — including the gating public-repo
guard — had been excluded, so the headline said nothing about them.

> The aggregate barely moved (46.1% → 45.6%) but it **hid a specific weak spot**:
> `check_local_paths` was the worst checker in the battery at 84.2%, with detection
> tested and *suppression* untested. Now 42.1% with the denominator unchanged. This is
> the argument against trusting aggregates: the mean was fine, the safety-critical
> component was not.

### The structural ceiling: 572 of 963 attribution lines unbindable

> `audit/BACKLOG.md:44` **bans quoting this as a percentage** of unverifiable
> attribution — it is an upper bound, and ~27 of the unbindable lines are not attribution
> claims at all. Treat it as a rough ceiling signal, not a statistic.

## 7. Standing hazard for whoever continues

Every error this session's reviews caught in my own work had one shape: **a confident
summary written without executing the thing that would settle it.** A fabricated
`module.gms:11,12` citation; a docstring asserting where four scalars live; a commit
metric that was not reproducible; "exactly one change" asserted three times without
diffing to the end of the file; a probe reporting all-zeros because it silently skipped a
string field; a rate quoted against the wrong base.

Every one was caught by *running something* — a checker, a diff, an independent agent.
None would have been caught by re-reading.
